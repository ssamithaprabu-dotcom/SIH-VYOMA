# MPU6050 6-Axis MotionTracking Device

## Overview
The MPU6050 combines a 3-axis gyroscope and a 3-axis accelerometer on the same silicon die together with an onboard Digital Motion Processor (DMP). Communicates over I2C bus.

## Operating Specifications
- Accelerometer Full Scale Range: ±2g, ±4g, ±8g, ±16g (configurable)
- Gyroscope Full Scale Range: ±250, ±500, ±1000, ±2000 deg/s
- Operating Voltage: 2.375V to 3.46V
- Default I2C Address: 0x68 (or 0x69 when AD0 pin HIGH)

## Diagnostic & Vibration Behavior
High acoustic vibration during rocket motor thrust burn can cause accelerometer saturation if the full-scale range is set below expected boost Gs. Abnormal acceleration spikes (>15g) during ASCENT may indicate structural mechanical resonance or motor thrust oscillations.
