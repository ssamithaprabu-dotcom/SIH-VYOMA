# BMP280 Digital Pressure Sensor

## Overview
The BMP280 is an absolute barometric pressure sensor designed for altitude tracking. Communicates via I2C or SPI interface.

## Operating Range
- Pressure Range: 300 hPa to 1100 hPa (equivalent to +9000m to -500m relative to sea level)
- Relative Accuracy: ±0.12 hPa (equivalent to ±1 meter)
- Absolute Accuracy: ±1.0 hPa

## Altitude Discrepancy Diagnostics
Dynamic pressure spikes caused by rocket venting, trans-sonic shockwaves, or unsealed avionics bay holes can cause temporary false barometric altitude spikes.
